HOW IT WORKS

Pick. Try on. Style. Share.

Four simple steps. No complicated editing.

01

Pick

Start with a photo or live camera

Choose a photo from your library or try lashes on live with your camera. A bright, front-facing photo looks most realistic.

02

Try on

Try 6 lash designs

Tap a design and slide the Intensity from lighter to bolder. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on the details.

03

Style

Make it yours

Switch between classic and volume, try quick presets and lash colors, and add color lenses. Tap Reset to go back to the default anytime.

04

Share

Save, share, or make a reel

Save in full quality, share a Story-ready image, send a salon style card to your lash artist, or create a Before → After reel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can I try with LashMuse?

Six lash designs, classic and volume lashes, lash colors, intensity from lighter to bolder, and color lenses, on your own photo or live through your camera.

Is LashMuse free?

Yes. All designs, lenses and full-quality saving are free, with no watermark. LashMuse is supported by ads.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Your photos and face data are processed only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to advertising partners.

Can I compare the result with my original photo?

Yes. Hold Before/after while editing, or drag the slider on the result screen.

Is LashMuse available for iPhone?

Yes. LashMuse is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Availability varies by region.
